**App name (30):** OffGrid Vault

**Short description (≤ 80):**
Offline password manager. No internet permission — your vault never leaves.

**Full description:**
OffGrid Vault is a password manager that never goes online. It doesn't just promise not to — it
has no internet permission at all, so it can't. You can check: look at "Permissions" on this page.

• Everything stays on this phone, encrypted with AES-256-GCM and keys held in Android's secure hardware
• Unlock with a 6-digit PIN or your fingerprint
• 12 recovery words, shown once, if you forget your PIN
• Autofill logins in apps and browsers — it checks the app's signature, so look-alike apps get nothing
• Saves new logins as you sign in, and keeps your last 5 passwords per login
• Password generator
• Locks itself when the screen turns off or after 5 minutes
• No account, no cloud, no sync, no backup, no ads, no analytics, no crash reports
• Screenshots and screen recording blocked inside the app
• Open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/mnvkalyansambhana-tech/offgrid-vault

Because nothing leaves the phone, uninstalling the app or losing the phone without recovery words
means the vault is gone. That's the deal: nobody else can ever get to it either.
